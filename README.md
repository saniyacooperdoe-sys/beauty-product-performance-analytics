# Beauty Product Performance Analytics

## Project Overview

This project analyzes a sample beauty product dataset to explore consumer ratings, pricing, product categories, and consumer engagement.

The goal is to use data analytics to answer business-focused questions about beauty product performance and demonstrate how data can support product and marketing decisions.

## Business Questions

1. Which beauty product categories have the highest average consumer ratings?
2. Is product price associated with consumer rating?
3. Which products generate the highest levels of consumer engagement?
4. What trends can be identified across beauty product categories?


## Key Findings

- Fragrance had the highest average consumer rating among the product categories analyzed, while Makeup generated the highest overall review activity.
- The relationship between product price and consumer rating showed a moderate positive correlation (r = 0.513), suggesting that higher-priced products in this sample tended to receive somewhat higher ratings.
- Maybelline Fit Me Foundation generated the highest consumer engagement with 18,400 reviews, followed by Lash Sensational Mascara with 15,200 reviews.
- High consumer engagement was not limited to premium-priced products. Several lower-priced makeup products generated substantially more reviews than higher-priced fragrance products.

## Business Takeaway

The analysis suggests that product performance should be evaluated using multiple measures rather than rating alone. While premium fragrance products received strong average ratings, lower-priced makeup products generated considerably more consumer engagement. Combining ratings, pricing, category, and review volume can provide a more complete view of product performance and consumer behavior.
